Delivery & Returns

Delivery Information

🚀

Standard Delivery

3–5 working days. £3.99 or FREE on orders over £30. Available to all UK mainland addresses via Royal Mail or DPD.

⚡

Express Delivery

Next working day when ordered before 2pm. £6.99 flat rate. Monday–Friday only, excludes bank holidays.

📦

Order Tracking

All orders include a tracking number sent via email once dispatched. Track your delivery via the carrier's website at any time.

Returns Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If for any reason you are not happy, we offer a straightforward 30-day returns policy.

Eligibility

Items must be returned within 30 days of receipt, unused, in their original packaging and in a saleable condition. For hygiene reasons, we are unable to accept returns on opened cosmetics or skincare products unless they are faulty.

How to Return

Contact our customer service team at returns@ipe-retail.co.uk with your order number and reason for return. We will provide a returns authorisation and instructions within 2 working days.

Refunds

Refunds are processed within 5–10 working days of receiving your returned item, issued to the original payment method. Return postage is at the customer's expense unless the item is faulty.

Faulty or Damaged Items

If you receive a faulty or damaged product, please contact us within 48 hours of receipt with photographic evidence. We will arrange a free return and issue a full refund or replacement.

Home / FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are all your products genuine? +
Yes — absolutely. Every product sold by IPE Retail Ltd is 100% genuine and sourced from authorised UK distributors or directly from brand owners. We do not stock grey market, parallel import or counterfeit goods.
Do you offer free delivery? +
Yes. Standard delivery is free on all orders over £30. Orders under £30 incur a flat £3.99 charge. Express next-day delivery is available for £6.99.
Can I return a product I'm not happy with? +
Yes. We offer a 30-day returns policy on all eligible items. Products must be unused and in original packaging. For hygiene reasons, opened cosmetics and skincare cannot be returned unless faulty. Contact returns@ipe-retail.co.uk to begin a return.
How do I track my order? +
Once your order is dispatched, you will receive an email with a tracking number. Use this on the carrier's website (Royal Mail or DPD) to follow your delivery in real time.
Do you supply trade customers or wholesalers? +
We do receive trade and wholesale enquiries and consider them on a case-by-case basis. Please use our Trade Enquiry form on the Contact page or email trade@ipe-retail.co.uk with details of your business and requirements.
Which brands do you stock? +
We currently stock: Neutrogena, Rimmel, Revlon, Maybelline, L'Oréal, Childs Farm, e.l.f., Max Factor, NIVEA, Bondi Sands, Wella, milk_shake and Joico. We are regularly adding new brands.
Where are you based? +
IPE Retail Ltd is a UK-registered company operating across multiple sales channels including this website and Amazon UK, serving customers throughout the United Kingdom.
How do I contact customer service? +
You can reach us via the Contact page or by emailing hello@ipe-retail.co.uk. We aim to respond to all enquiries within 1 working day.
